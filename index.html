01Problem

Compliance in Nigeria is broken by design

3.1 million registered businesses. 245,000 new ones every year. Almost none of them have a system tracking what they legally owe.

Agents sell a certificate and vanish the moment it's issued — no guidance on what comes next.

Lawyers are too expensive for most founders to afford in the first place, so legal advice never enters the picture at all.

Founders discover obligations like SCUML, NDPR, trademarks, and annual returns only after they've already missed them — usually at the worst possible moment, mid-raise or mid-audit.

  • Agents disappear after the certificate

    No guidance on what legal obligations follow registration.

  • Lawyers are unaffordable at scale

    Most Nigerian founders simply can't afford one in the first place.

  • Hidden obligations surface too late

    SCUML, NDPR, and trademark gaps appear only after enforcement.

  • No infrastructure to track compliance

    Missed filings quietly accumulate fines with CAC — until a founder finds out during due diligence.
